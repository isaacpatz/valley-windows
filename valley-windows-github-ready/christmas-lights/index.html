Good to know

Christmas light questions, answered.

How much does it cost?+

Every display is custom, so pricing depends on your home and the time of season. We do have a project minimum. Fill out the quote form or give us a call at (256) 217-5335 and we'll get you an exact price fast.

Can you install my own lights?+

No. We only install our own commercial-grade lights so we can guarantee quality and keep everything covered.

Do you provide the Christmas lights?+

Yes. Commercial-grade lights, clips, cords, and timers are all included.

What happens if a light stops working?+

Maintenance is included all season. Text or call us and we'll come get it shining again.

When do you install and take them down?+

Installs start in October and run through the season. Takedown happens in January.

What colors can I choose?+

We offer a wide range of options, including warm white, multicolor, candy cane, and custom color combinations designed to match your home and style.

Will installation damage my house?+

No. We strategically place every clip and strand so nothing is nailed or forced. When we take it all down, no damage is left behind.
