About The Valley Window Services

ABOUT THE
VALLEY WINDOW SERVICES

Founded in Huntsville, Alabama, Valley Window Services began with two brothers and a simple goal: bring honesty, quality work, and real customer care back to service businesses. Inspired by the community of Jones Valley where we grew up, we take pride in serving our neighbors across Huntsville and the surrounding areas. What started with just a squeegee and mop cleaning one-story homes has grown into a professional window cleaning service capable of safely cleaning homes up to three stories. Along with window cleaning, we offer gutter cleaning, screen cleaning, and Christmas light installation. Our mission is simple: deliver spotless results while treating every customer with professionalism, clear communication, and true Southern hospitality.

Our 4 Step Process

OUR 4 STEP PROCESS

with Rain Guard.

01

Scrub

We begin with a thorough pre-cleaning process, removing dirt, grime, and stubborn residue. Using professional-grade solutions and specialized scrubbing techniques, we ensure every inch of your window surface is perfectly prepared for the next step.

02

Squeegee

Our expert technicians employ precise squeegee techniques perfected over years of experience. With smooth, continuous strokes, we remove every drop of cleaning solution, leaving behind crystal-clear glass without streaks or water marks.

03

Detail

Perfection is in the details. We meticulously clean window frames, sills, and corners that others overlook. Every edge is polished, every surface inspected, ensuring your windows look flawless from every angle and truly shine.

04

Rain Guard

Our protective coating creates an invisible barrier that repels water, dirt, and pollution—keeping windows cleaner for longer and reducing maintenance needs while enhancing clarity.
